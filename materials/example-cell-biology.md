# Example notes: Cell Biology (replace with your own)

## Cell structure
- **Prokaryotes** (bacteria, archaea) have no nucleus; DNA sits in the nucleoid.
- **Eukaryotes** have a membrane-bound nucleus and organelles.
- **Mitochondria** make ATP through cellular respiration; they have their own DNA.
- **Chloroplasts** (plants, algae) carry out photosynthesis.
- The **plasma membrane** is a phospholipid bilayer; it is selectively permeable.

## Cellular respiration
1. **Glycolysis** (cytoplasm): glucose -> 2 pyruvate, net 2 ATP, 2 NADH.
2. **Krebs / citric acid cycle** (mitochondrial matrix): produces NADH, FADH2, CO2.
3. **Electron transport chain** (inner mitochondrial membrane): uses NADH/FADH2 to
   pump protons; ATP synthase makes ~26-28 ATP. Oxygen is the final electron acceptor.

## Cell division
- **Mitosis**: one division -> 2 identical diploid cells. Phases: prophase,
  metaphase, anaphase, telophase (PMAT), then cytokinesis.
- **Meiosis**: two divisions -> 4 genetically different haploid gametes.
  Crossing over happens in prophase I; homologs separate in anaphase I.
